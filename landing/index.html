Questions

Is this run by the University of Miami?

No. CanesXchange was built by a UM student as an independent project. It is not officially run or endorsed by the University of Miami. UM email is used only to confirm you are a student.

What happens after I sign up?

We are launching building by building. If you say yes, we'll email you when CanesXchange launches in your building.

Do you check item condition?

No. Verification confirms the seller is a UM student, not the condition of the item. Sofas and other upholstery need a dated condition note from the seller.

What do you do with my information?

Your email and building are used only to contact you about CanesXchange. We never publish unit numbers and we delete move out details at the end of each season.
